## 1. Setup Overview & Component Alignment

The image intensifier was powered on in strict compliance with the Standard Operating Procedure (SOP). The optical axis height was realigned across all components.

* **Optical Components:** Target, 75 mm lens, Image Intensifier, 50 mm lens and capture camera.

## 2. Image Intensifier Gain Calibration

The gain regulator was swept from **5.0 V down to 0.0 V**:
* **Threshold Voltage:** The target pattern first became visible on the camera at **2.1 V** gain.
* **Captures Taken:** Dedicated reference images were recorded at gain settings of **2.0 V, 1.5 V, 1.0 V, 0.5 V, and 0.0 V**.

![Phosphor Screen Image Output](:/ebee06fce367487590d1b0b0256b0c9c)

---

## 3. Distance Sweep & Resolution Optimization

Starting at 11:25 AM, the target was moved backwards along the optical axis in **5 mm increments**:
* **Maximum Distance Reached:** Target object placed at **37.2 cm** from the base of the 75 mm lens.
* **Optimal Focusing Point:** The highest image sharpness and resolution were achieved with the target at **362 mm (36.2 cm)** from the base of the 75 mm lens.

![Best Resolution Capture - CRAB Setup](:/2ba265c244dc4bc295108936d6d52b69)
